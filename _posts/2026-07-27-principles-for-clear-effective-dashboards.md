Dashboards are commonly used to visualize data. They are especially useful when you want to track metrics on a regular basis, for example, track sales of product every month. Moreover, a visual approach is often the quickest way to find outliers. Here is a sample dashboard that I designed using Tableau for a laptop retailer in my visualization class. The dashboard tracks monthly sales of different laptop models across all its retail stores. The features section helps the company to keep a track of what laptop features are popular among its customer.
